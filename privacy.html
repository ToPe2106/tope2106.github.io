Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

Youtube hustle uploader is a private tool. Its only user is the owner of the channels it uploads to. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channels, and to read basic channel information (channel ID, name and the publish times of scheduled videos) so it uploads to the right channel at the right time.

What it stores

An access token is stored only in the owner's private server environment. A log of uploads (video ID, title, channel and publish time) is kept for the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
